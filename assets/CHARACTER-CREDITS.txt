The source pages declare these Innerscene/MakeHuman/MPFB derivatives CC0.
Project modifications: geometry reduction, removal of unused hidden/accessory
parts, 512-pixel texture resizing/compression, animation-channel transfer,
embedded GLB data and scene placement. These are generic digital characters,
not likenesses of Robina or of the fictional gala nominees. The source motions
are authored animation, not motion capture.

Three.js r160.1 GLTFLoader, BufferGeometryUtils and SkeletonUtils use the engine's
MIT license, included as THREE-LICENSE.txt. Their module wrappers were adapted
to share the bundled global Three.js instance; no CDN is needed at runtime.
